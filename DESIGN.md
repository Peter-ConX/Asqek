## Brand & Style

This design system expresses high-conviction engineering authority tailored for a premium web development studio. The visual language bridges elite SaaS product rigor with high-converting agency polish. It targets enterprise decision-makers, technical founders, and growth-stage product teams who prioritize performance, modern engineering standards, and bespoke aesthetic refinement over generic templates.

The aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** precision with **Refined Minimalism**:
- Crisp visual hierarchy anchored by razor-sharp typography and measured whitespace.
- Restrained color distribution: functional neutrality commands the canvas while deep royal blue directs action and emerald green validates growth, performance, and operational velocity.
- Micro-elevations and light-capturing surfaces communicate dimensional craftsmanship without decorative visual noise.
- Every viewport projects technological discipline, reliability, and undeniable production competency.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of reliability, clarity, and technical excellence through a light-first configuration.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Primary (`#1652F0`)**: Royal Blue serves as the core action driver, high-intent interactive element, and focal identity color. Use for primary CTAs, active states, key data accents, and anchor branding elements.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#17B978`)**: Emerald Green functions as a conversion and confirmation catalyst. Reserved for metric highlights, conversion triggers, uptime indicators, verified badges, and successful deployment states.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Slate/Navy represents maximum text contrast, authoritative headings, and high-impact structural anchors.
- **Canvas Tiers**:
  - Base Surface: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) ensures optimal readability and modern crispness.
  - Sub-surfaces & Sections: Light Slate (`#F8FAFC`) and Muted Zinc (`#F1F5F9`) define alternating content bands, nested component wells, and subtle card backdrops.
- **Structural Lines (`#E2E8F0`)**: A calibrated slate hairline border delineates card edges, table dividers, and structural grids without visually interrupting user focus.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the sculptural confidence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and brand-defining actions with the functional precision of **Inter** for sustained technical body copy.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set tightly with negative letter tracking to produce a cohesive, punchy editorial posture. Leading is deliberately compact to prevent fragmented line wraps in hero sections.
- **Body Elements**: Set in Inter with generous line heights to facilitate fluid eye-tracking across technical descriptions, service outlines, and value propositions.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans with distinct medium-to-bold weights. `label-sm` utilizes an expanded tracking (`0.04em`) when applied as uppercase category badges or service metadata tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive 12-column grid bounded by a central max-width of `1280px` for standard content and `1440px` for high-impact visual showcases.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (1024px and above)**: Full 12-column grid utilizing a standard `gutter` of 1.5rem and section margins of 3rem. Service comparison matrices and case study modules utilize 2, 3, 4, or 6 column spans.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter-tablet` (1.25rem) and margins of 2rem. Three-column desktop cards reflow to a 2x2 grid or stacked pairs.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile` (1rem) and tight 1.25rem outer canvas margins. Multi-column cards stack vertically into unified full-width modules.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks are spaced using rhythmic multiples of `space-xl` (e.g., 5rem to 7.5rem padding between marketing landing blocks) to ensure content breathes naturally and distinct agency capabilities are consumed sequentially.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on ambient layered depth rather than sharp, heavy shadows. Components sit on subtle z-axis planes that evoke precision and tactile quality.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Flat Baseline**: Raw canvas surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with zero elevation.
- **Card Surface Level 1 (Resting)**:
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
- **Card Surface Level 2 (Hover / Interactive Focus)**:
  - Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1` with optional subtle transition toward `#1652F0` opacity tint
  - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07)`
  - Transform: `-2px` translateY on interactive agency portfolio and service offering cards.
- **Floating Overlays & Modals**:
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - Border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)
- **Glass Accents**: Sticky navigation headers feature an active backdrop-filter of `blur(12px)` combined with an 80% opacity fill of `#FFFFFF` and a bottom hairline border of `#E2E8F0`.

## Shapes

The geometric personality features balanced, contemporary rounding. It maintains software-like authority while remaining friendly and ergonomic.

- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Standardized on `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for compact elements, and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for hero containers, large service banners, and featured project callouts.
- **Interactive Controls**: Primary and secondary buttons, input fields, and search modules use 0.5rem (8px) to 0.75rem (12px) radiuses to preserve an architectural silhouette.
- **Status Badges & Chips**: Utilize full pill geometry (`9999px`) to create clear shape contrast against rectangular card surfaces.
- **Icon Enclosures**: Contained inside soft squircle backgrounds (0.75rem / 12px radius) with subtle tint backgrounds of `#F1F5F9` or 10% opacity tints of the primary blue and emerald green.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#1652F0`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`, radius 0.5rem to 0.75rem. Inner box-shadow highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: `#1244C5` with subtle elevation increase.
- **Accent (Conversion High-Priority)**: Background `#17B978`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`. Designed for schedule/consultation actions. On hover: `#139D66`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: Background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Text**: Transparent background, text `#1652F0`, font `label-md`. Includes an animated trailing arrow glyph moving 4px to the right on hover.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Standard Service Card**: White background with 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border and Level 1 resting shadow. Corner radius `rounded-lg` (16px) or `rounded-xl` (24px). Internal padding `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- **Featured / Tier Highlight Card**: Elevated with an active 2px border in `#1652F0` or featuring an ambient badge in top right corner. Uses a subtle background gradient running from `#FFFFFF` down to `#F8FAFC`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicator**: Pill-shaped container (`9999px`), 0.25rem vertical and 0.75rem horizontal padding. Features an active pulsing dot (6px emerald green circle). Background tint is 10% alpha of `#17B978` with saturated text.
- **Category Tags**: Slate tint background (`#F1F5F9`), text `#0F172A`, font `label-sm`, tracking `0.04em`, uppercase.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs**: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, radius 0.5rem, height 48px. Placeholder color `#94A3B8`. Focused state: 2px solid `#1652F0` ring with a 2px offset in `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 1.25rem squares or circles, border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#1652F0` displaying a crisp white icon check.

### Code & Architecture Snippets
- Dedicated technological proof components utilizing monospaced accents (`JetBrains Mono`), dark slate backgrounds (`#0F172A`), syntax-highlighted accents in `#17B978` and `#1652F0`, enclosed in `rounded-lg` containers with an integrated micro-header showing file paths and mock window controls.

### Metric / KPI Showcases
- Large numerical counters using `display-hero` sizing in `#0F172A` paired with an adjacent upward trend pill in `#17B978`, stacked above a concise two-line explanation in `body-sm`.
